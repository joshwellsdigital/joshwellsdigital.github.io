Frequently asked questions

Will my website actually show up on Google? How does SEO work here?
Great question. "Showing up on Google" isn't automatic — but we don't just build a site and disappear. Every site we deliver includes on-page SEO basics: meta tags, heading structure, image alt text, fast load times, mobile responsiveness, and schema markup. For ongoing visibility, we recommend a monthly SEO package (local SEO, content updates, backlink strategy). We're transparent: SEO is a marathon, not a sprint.
Do you provide ongoing support & maintenance after launch?
Yes — but it's not locked into a mandatory contract like some agencies. Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support (bug fixes, minor tweaks). After that, you can choose a monthly maintenance plan (updates, backups, security monitoring, content changes) or pay as you go. No hard sell. We want you to run your business, not worry about tech.
Do I need to own my domain and hosting? What happens if we stop working together?
You own everything. We help you set up hosting under your own account (or we can manage it with your permission). If we ever part ways, you keep the website files, domain, content, and SEO progress. No hostage situations — that's bad business.
Can you work with an existing website, or do we need a full rebuild?
Either way — it depends on your goals. If your current site is slow, not mobile-friendly, or hurting your SEO, a rebuild often saves you money long-term. If the structure is solid but needs design or content updates, we can do a refresh. We'll audit first and give you honest advice, not upsell what you don't need.
What's the difference between web design, SEO, and digital marketing from you?
Web design = your storefront (look, feel, user experience).
SEO = getting people to find that storefront (search rankings, traffic).
Digital marketing = ongoing strategy (ads, email, social, content).

Many clients start with design + SEO, then add marketing later. We can do any combination — no pressure to buy a bundle you don't need.
